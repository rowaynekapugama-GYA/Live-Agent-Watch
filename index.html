One site's sighting becomes every site's warning

Every watched site sends a short note about each visit. Agent Watch rates how far each AI agent went and compares notes with every other site. When the same agent reaches level 3 on 3 or more unrelated sites within 2 hours, it's a campaign, and owners of similar sites are warned.

1 · ReadRead public pages. Shown on the dashboard.
2 · MapListed the site's pages, forms and entry points. Shown on the dashboard.
3 · ProbeTested forms, logins or limits. Email alert.
4 · AttemptTried a known break-in trick. Urgent email alert.
5 · Got inOwner confirms what happened. Set by hand for now.

What we say, and what we guess

If an agent names itself, we show that. If not, it's marked "possible". We report, we don't block; the owner decides. We never see what visitors type, read or upload, and addresses are scrambled the moment they arrive.
